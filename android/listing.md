# The store listing

The words of Behold's main store listing on Google Play, in English (United States) (Gate 17 A8,
#68). Each field goes into Play Console as its block stands.

- **The app name** is the web manifest's.
- **The short description** is the title's line (Gate 20's note on #68).
- **The full description** is the owner's ruling of 2026-10-15 (#68): approved copy, naming no
  translation. Each case's PR added its title and brief, and 67a, the season's last, made it
  complete.

`src/android.test.ts` holds each field to Play's limit, the name to the manifest the build writes,
the short description to the title's line, the full description to every case's title and brief,
and all three to naming no translation the reveal marks.

The listing's graphics are in `assets/`: the store's icon and the feature graphic
(`docs/icon.md`).

## App name

```text
Behold: Bible Mystery Game
```

## Short description

```text
Look closer. There's more to every story.
```

## Full description

```text
Behold is a mystery game set in the stories of the Bible. Each case is a passage: you study the pictures of its moments, work out who is who and what happened, and fill the blanks of the account in the game’s own words — then the reveal shows the text itself.

Tap anything that looks like it matters. You don’t need to remember the story. Everything you need is in the picture.

Start with the valley. It teaches the game. A giant lies face-down in a valley, and a boy with a sling stands near him. Who are they, and how does it end?

Season one, the house of Ahab, is complete. The story runs in order.

The mountain
Two altars on a mountain, a bull on each, and all Israel watching. What did the people answer, what did the fire burn, and what did the people do then?

The vineyard
A man walks a vineyard as if it were his, and a prophet has come to meet him. Whose vineyard was it, and what became of its owner?

The battle
A crowd of prophets tells a king to go to war, and one plain man is sent to prison. Then two kings ride into battle, and only one of them is dressed as a king. Who is the man in plain armor, and what became of his chariot?

The room
A woman builds a small room on her roof for a holy man who often comes her way. Years later she rides hard to find him, and tells all who ask that everything is all right. Whose room is it, and what happened on its bed?

The gift
A commander of Aram comes to Israel’s prophet with leprosy, and goes home clean. Then the prophet’s servant runs after the commander, with an answer for everyone who asks. Who sent him, and where had he been?

The window
Years after a prophet’s word against a king, spoken in a vineyard, a commander is anointed in secret and sets out for Jezreel. Two kings ride out to meet him, and a queen watches from her window. On whom was the word kept, and how?

Stuck? Ask for a hint: Where to look, then Show me.

Best with sound on.

Behold has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics, and it collects nothing about you. Your progress stays on your device, and the game never sends it anywhere.
```
